Mission Awareness Foundation

The Beginning of Positive Changes..

Empowering Students Through Education and Comprehensive Support Services.

Abdur Rehman Khan, Founder

Mission Awareness Foundation (MAF), established in 2020, is a non-profit organization led by socially responsible individuals. MAF supports marginalized communities through programs that combat child labor and promote education, women’s empowerment, youth development, and overall community progress.

Employment

Empowering careers through skill development and hands-on learning

At the heart of our employment initiatives lies the Mumbai Coding Club, established in 2021. This institute is dedicated to nurturing talent by offering hands-on coding education, transforming students into Python Full Stack Developers. With personalized one-to-one guidance and experienced mentors, we ensure every student receives the support they need to excel.

Achievements

Over 40+ students successfully placed since inception.

Personalized mentorship tailored to individual learning needs.

Hands-on experience with real-world projects to bridge the gap between learning and application.

Our program doesn’t just end with skill development. We make students job-ready through practical training, resume building, and mock interviews. Students also gain invaluable experience by working on real-world projects, ensuring they are equipped with practical knowledge and industry-relevant skills. We provide full job assistance, connecting them with top opportunities in the industry.
